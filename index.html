Extra Lab · Repetition

Applies to Unit Digit, Digital Root, Near-base, Percentage, Estimation and Mixed Challenge.

Quick Formula Desk

Unit digit: powers repeat in cycles of at most 4. Reduce the exponent using the cycle length.

Digital root: repeatedly add digits; for a positive integer, the result is congruent modulo 9.

Near 100: (100−a)(100−b) = (100−a−b) × 100 + ab.

Percentage: x% of y = y% of x. 25% = ÷4, 12.5% = ÷8, 5% = ÷20.

Estimation: round to compatible numbers, estimate, then check whether the options are sufficiently separated.

Use exact calculation when the question asks for an exact value; estimation is only for suitable questions.
